| 2026-11-28 | Table cells take a selection and formatting: dragging, a double click for a word, a triple click for a line, Shift and the arrow keys, Shift and a click, or Ctrl+A select the text of the cell being typed in (`table.cellSelect`, `table.cellSelectAll`); the ribbon and Ctrl+B/I/U format it (`format.*` acts in the cell), alignment aligns the cell, and typing, Enter, Backspace, Delete and a paste replace the selection; Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X put the selected cell text on the clipboard as HTML and text (`table.cellCopy`, `table.cellCut`). A cell's edits are now undo steps of their own (they went around the undo history before). A picture copied in a browser or a file manager pastes above the cell's text. Copying a message in the reading pane writes its selected text as HTML with its look beside the plain text, so a word processor or another mail keeps the formatting. Nested tables, lists and several pictures in one cell are still the next step |
