- **A picture above the text of a table cell.** Pasting a picture with the caret in a table cell puts it above that cell's text; the cell is laid out as tall as the picture and its words, the picture is sent with the message or signature (as its own inline part, as a picture in the text is), and it is kept in a draft or signature. (A cell still holds one picture and its text; nested tables, lists and several pictures in one cell are the next step.)
