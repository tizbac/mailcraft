- **A table cell can be typed in.** Clicking a cell puts the one caret in it (the cell is highlighted, and the message's own caret is not drawn beside it), and typing, Enter, Backspace/Delete, Left/Right and a paste go into that cell; Escape or a click elsewhere leaves it. Insert › Table starts in its first cell. The cell's text, lines and styles are kept in the draft or signature and sent as the cell's own text.
