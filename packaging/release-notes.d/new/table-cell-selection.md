- **Select and format the text of a table cell.** A table cell's text is selected as the message's is — by dragging, a double click for a word, a triple click for a line, Shift and the arrow keys, Shift and a click, or Ctrl+A — and the ribbon and the keys format exactly what is selected: bold, italic, underline, font, size, colour, highlight, Clear All Formatting, and a cell's alignment. Typing, Enter, Backspace, Delete or a paste replaces the selection, and Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X put it on the clipboard as HTML and text so its formatting is kept in another mail or a word processor. The cell's text selection is drawn behind it, and the ribbon shows the look of what is selected.
