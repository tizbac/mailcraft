- **A mail account that signs in with a password on the phone.** Add Account now offers an IMAP account: the address, the password, the IMAP server and the SMTP server (their usual names and ports are filled in from the address, and can be changed). The phone reads its mail, sends through its SMTP server, keeps the password in the phone's secure store when sign-ins are remembered, and sends and receives it at the same times as a Microsoft account's mail. Exchange and Linchange accounts are still to come.
